BEST PRACTICES

Cybersecurity Defense Tips

Actionable everyday habits to secure enterprise data and protect workstations.

1. Use Strong Passphrases

Combine 4+ random words like 'sunset-battery-falcon-origami'. Length exponentially beats short complexity against brute-force attacks.

2. Enable Multi-Factor Authentication

MFA blocks 99.9% of automated account breaches. Prefer authenticator apps (TOTP) or hardware keys over SMS.

3. Don't Click Suspicious Links

Always hover over links to preview the actual destination URL before clicking. Inspect lookalike characters carefully.

4. Keep Software Updated

Enable automatic updates for your OS, browser, and security agents to patch known vulnerabilities promptly.

5. Lock Your Computer

Whenever leaving your desk, press Win+L or Cmd+Ctrl+Q. Never leave sensitive documents or screens exposed.

6. Avoid Public Wi-Fi Without VPN

Public Wi-Fi at airports and cafes is vulnerable to packet sniffing. Always route traffic through the enterprise VPN.
